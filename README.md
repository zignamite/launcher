## 🔧 Frequently Asked Questions

### ❓ Do I need an API key?

No. aiusagebar works with your regular ChatGPT or Claude account, so there's nothing extra to set up. The app reads your usage display directly from your connected accounts.

### ❓ Is my data private?

Yes. All of your usage information stays on your computer. aiusagebar doesn't store or transmit your personal data anywhere.

### ❓ Why are my rings showing zero?

If you haven't used any AI services yet today, your rings will correctly display zero usage. Start using ChatGPT or Claude, and you'll see your rings start to fill up.

### ❓ Can I use aiusagebar with multiple accounts?

Yes, aiusagebar can remember multiple accounts. Use the settings menu to log in to different accounts, and switch between them whenever you need.

### ❓ Does aiusagebar work with AI coding assistants?

Absolutely. The app also tracks Codex and Claude Code usage, so you have full visibility across all your AI tools.

### ❓ What happens when my limit is reached?

Your ring will turn solid red, and you'll see a notification. The app will continue showing you how much time is left until your quota resets, so you know when you can start using AI again.

## 📖 Troubleshooting

### The app won't start

- Check your internet connection
- Make sure you aren't already running another instance of aiusagebar
- Restart your computer and try again

### The rings are not appearing

- Right-click the icon and select "Refresh"
- Make sure you're logged in to ChatGPT or Claude in your browser
- If you're using a work account, your organization may restrict usage visibility

### The numbers seem wrong

- Wait a few moments – aiusagebar syncs in the background
- Check that your system clock is set correctly
- Log out and back in from the aiusagebar settings
